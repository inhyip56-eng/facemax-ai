Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FaceMax AI: Glow Up Lab ("we," "our," or "us") handles data within the FaceMax AI: Glow Up Lab mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure user experience.

1. Photo Data and Storage

The App is designed with privacy at its core.

  • AI Analysis via Server: When you perform a face scan, a resized thumbnail (max 768px) is sent to our secure backend and analyzed by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite AI (accessed via OpenRouter, a secure API gateway, routed to Google Vertex AI infrastructure in the EU). Your full-resolution photo never leaves your device. The thumbnail is processed in real-time and not stored after the response is returned.
  • Analysis vs. Backup: Full-resolution face photos are not stored by FaceMax. The resized image sent for AI analysis is processed in real time and discarded after the response. If you sign in with Apple, FaceMax may separately store a small compressed history thumbnail for each scan in a private Cloudflare D1 table so your Scan History can be restored on another device.

2. Information We Process

To provide you with personalized style and grooming recommendations, we process the following data:

  • Anonymous Facial Metrics: The App extracts non-identifiable, purely mathematical metrics of your facial structure (such as jawline angles, symmetry ratios, and facial width-to-height ratio).
  • Processing via AI: Numeric metrics are sent to our secure backend and processed by AI to generate your report. For Premium Glow Up Plans, a face thumbnail may also be included to improve visual accuracy. No raw biometric data is retained.
  • Apple Account: FaceMax uses Sign in with Apple for its account-based iOS experience so progress and Premium can stay linked across iPhones. Your FaceMax account identifier is derived from Apple’s stable user identifier. FaceMax does not request your Apple name or email for this feature.

3. Food Scanner

The App includes a food scanner feature that analyzes photos of meals for nutritional and bloating information.

  • Food Photos Only: When you use the food scanner, the photo of your meal is sent to our secure backend for analysis. These photos are processed in real-time and are not stored.
  • Progress Backup: After Sign in with Apple, recent food-scan results and daily food-log progress may be backed up. FaceMax may also store a small compressed meal-history thumbnail in a private Cloudflare D1 table so Food Scan history can be restored on another device. The original/full-resolution meal photo is not stored as part of cloud backup.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party AI services to generate face analysis reports and food analysis. Specifically, we use Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, accessed via OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) — a secure API gateway that routes requests to Google Vertex AI infrastructure located in the European Union (EU). For every face scan, a resized thumbnail (max 768 px) and anonymous numeric facial metrics are sent for real-time processing. For Glow Up Plan generation, the same data is used to produce a personalised plan. Food scan photos are processed separately via the same pipeline. No personal identifiers, names, or account information are shared with third parties.

Before your first photo is sent for analysis, the App displays an in-app consent screen that names the third-party AI service (Google Gemini, via OpenRouter), describes what is sent, and requires you to actively check a box and tap "Agree & continue" before any photo leaves your device. You may decline, in which case the photo will not be sent and the scan will not proceed.

5. Sign in with Apple and Cloud Sync

FaceMax uses Sign in with Apple to authenticate the account-based iOS experience and keep progress and Premium associated with one FaceMax account across iPhones. The onboarding quiz may be viewed before authentication, but an Apple account is required before entering the signed-in app experience or purchasing Premium.

  • What we receive: An Apple identity token and stable Apple user identifier used to create a FaceMax account ID. FaceMax does not request your Apple name or email for cloud sync.
  • What is backed up: Face Scan scores and metrics, goals and preferences, Glow Up Plan state, streaks, achievements, meal-plan progress, and similar app progress data.
  • Cloud media backup: D1 stores progress data and small compressed Face Scan and Food Scan history thumbnails in a private database table. Original/full-resolution scan photos and your profile picture are not included in this cloud media backup.
  • Where progress is stored: Synced progress and small history thumbnails are stored using Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1. Thumbnails are kept in a separate private D1 table. Both are associated with your FaceMax account ID and are accessed through authenticated FaceMax API requests.
  • Account deletion: You can use Delete Account in Profile to delete account-linked cloud data. Deleting the FaceMax account does not by itself cancel an App Store subscription; subscription management remains available through Apple.

6. Analytics and In-App Purchases

  • Analytics and App Usage: We may collect anonymous, non-identifying data regarding how you use the App (e.g., crash reports, feature usage) to improve our services. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • In-App Purchases: If you purchase a Premium subscription, your payment is processed securely by Apple (App Store). We do not have access to your credit card information. We only receive a secure, anonymous token to verify your subscription status.

7. Subscriptions and Auto-Renewal

FaceMax AI: Glow Up Lab offers auto-renewable subscription plans (weekly, monthly, and yearly) that provide access to Premium features.

  • Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.
  • Billing: Your Apple ID account will be charged upon purchase confirmation and at the start of each renewal period.
  • Cancellation: You can cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • No Refunds: Unused portions of a free trial period, if offered, are forfeited when a subscription is purchased. Refund requests must be submitted to Apple directly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will promptly delete their data.

9. GDPR (Europe) and CCPA (USA) Compliance

We take your privacy rights seriously. Here is what we store and your rights regarding that data:

  • What we store: Synced progress is associated with your FaceMax account ID derived from Apple’s stable user identifier. Some pre-account versions of FaceMax may also have limited backend records associated with a device-generated User ID during migration. Cloud backup may include small Face Scan and Food Scan history thumbnails; original/full-resolution scan photos and profile pictures are excluded.
  • Right to deletion: You can delete all your data at any time via the Delete Account option in the Profile tab, or by emailing us at privacy@facemaxaiapp.com. All data is permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Right to access: You may request a copy of your stored data by contacting us at the email above.
  • No selling of data: We do not sell your data to third parties.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
